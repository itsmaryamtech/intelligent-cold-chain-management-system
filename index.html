    About the Technology

    Open, rule-based, integration-ready

    The target architecture is a React and TypeScript frontend with a FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend, role-based access control and REST APIs. Sensors, gateways, GPS devices, email and SMS providers, and ERP or WMS systems can be connected later. This preview is frontend only: no hardware, backend or third-party service is active.
